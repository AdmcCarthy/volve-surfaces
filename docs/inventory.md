## Findings

- All 32 horizon files parse: five numeric columns, no null sentinels, no NaN. Every file also has 4 to 6 non-`#` metadata lines (survey, horizon name, source, domain), skipped by the row filter.
- IL and XL steps are 1 (one file has IL step 2): a regular index grid. Official surfaces are 88 to 100 % filled, so holes (fault gaps, edges) must be masked, not interpolated across silently.
- Depth surfaces are positive down; Hugin Top spans 2,723 to 3,394 m, Base 2,741 to 3,487 m. The datum (MSL or other) is not stated in the files: treat as an open question.
- **`Seabed+VelBuild_04_08+PUB+DEPTH.dat` holds two surfaces.** Rows 1 to 280,641 are the seabed (83 to about 130 m); the rest (235,613 rows, Z 2,410 to 2,686) match the Hod TWT horizon and are not seabed depth. The build must split sections and keep only the first.
- Header CRS: `ST_ED50_UTM31N_P23031_T1133` on most files; the Multiples and "File creator = knutm" files omit it. Assume EPSG:23031 for all, flagged.
- The area is small: X 430 to 440 km, Y 6,475 to 6,482 km (about 9 by 7 km). Grids at 12.5 by 25 m nodes are far more detail than a map needs.
- Timelapse (4D) horizons are patches (33 to 48 % fill, 2 to 12 k nodes): map them as overlays, not full surfaces.
