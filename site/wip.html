Hydrocarbon contacts: not part of the base case

The repository is about rendering surfaces from seismic interpretations. These maps are an experiment on top of it: a hydrocarbon contact fill, per fault segment, with the Volve evidence. They are unfinished, partly digitised from a figure, and paused. Do not read them as results. The closure analysis and area-against-depth charts live in the viewer under the same heading.

The ramps

Sequential ramps (seven steps) for depth and thickness, and diverging ramps (nine steps) for residuals. Steps are read from the live tokens; the stepped fills use them directly, the linear fills blend between them in Lab.
